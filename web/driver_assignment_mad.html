5. Result

"Unassigned tasks" = the rest-time rule (the only hard limit -- never bent) ruled out every available driver that day, or there were simply more tasks than available drivers. Shift-lock is a soft preference: a driver outside their locked shift still gets picked over leaving a task unfilled. "Unassigned drivers" should always read 0 -- any driver left over once every real task is handled gets a manufactured "RESERVE" duty for the day (written into their roster cell, in their own locked shift's window) instead of being left blank, so no available driver ever shows up with nothing to do.
