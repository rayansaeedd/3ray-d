5. Day-by-day generation

Loading builds this session from the files/dates/conditions above. Pick a day, Generate it, review the counts below, then Grant it once you're happy -- only granted days feed the next day's Generate. You can Ungrant a day to edit or re-generate it.
"Unassigned tasks" = the rest-time rule (the only hard limit) ruled out every driver in this task's own locked shift and the shift(s) immediately next to it, or there were simply more tasks than available drivers. "Unassigned drivers" is left genuinely blank on purpose -- no manufactured reserve duty -- for you to fix by hand (that editing comes in a later step of this build).
